Arco microphone test

This uses Arco’s local English recognizer and speech detection. It displays what it hears and the command it recognizes. Commands here have no effect on workers. Luna is not used.

Say “Computer” and wait for the computer acknowledgement and green capture indicator. Take your time before speaking or between thoughts: capture stays armed for two minutes of silence. To submit, pause and say “over” on its own (“execute”, “engage”, “make it so” also work); to discard, say “cancel” on its own. Inside a sentence those words are ordinary text. “End capture” / “Cancel capture” still work.

After capture starts, “computer” is ordinary text. Saying it again keeps the existing capture; it does not restart or erase your words.

Voice audition

All voices run locally. GPU engines load their model per sample (first run downloads it), so expect a wait; the timing line shows load vs. generate. The TNG clone needs your own clip at ~/.archon/voice/reference/tng-computer.wav (10–20 s, clean, plus tng-computer.txt with its transcript). Stop the microphone before auditioning.

Higher sensitivity can pick up softer speech and more room noise. Change this while listening.

Try these phrases

  1. Say just “Computer”. Wait for the acknowledgement and “CAPTURE ARMED”.
  2. Wait 10 seconds. The capture indicator should stay green.
  3. “Open the plan.” Pause again as needed.
  4. Pause, then “Over.” — capture closes and the test shows which action would run. Try “move it over” first: it must stay literal.
  5. “Computer, show member two, end capture” also works in one breath. “Cancel capture” discards an armed capture.
  6. “Computer, stop listening.” — this also switches this test’s microphone off.

Only the latest recognition is displayed. No audio or transcript is saved. Closing or reloading this page turns the microphone off. Recognition prepares before listening begins.
